7 tasks you can automate today

SaraSep 20, 2026 • 5 min read

A practical list of repetitive tasks eating your team’s time, and how to drop them.

1. Sending weekly reports

Instead of collecting numbers every Thursday, let the dashboard email a PDF report on a fixed schedule.

2. Following up on leads

Every new contact form can create a task and send a welcome message with no manual step.

The best automation is the one nobody notices because it always works.

3. Stock alerts

When an item drops below its minimum, the purchasing channel gets an alert right away.

  • Pick the repetitive task
  • Choose the trigger and action
  • Test it, then turn it on
