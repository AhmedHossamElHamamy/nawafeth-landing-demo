Brand identity for a local café

How we helped a new café build a distinctive identity that drew new customers and three new branches.

ClientLocal café
ServiceBranding
Duration4 weeks
Year2026
The challenge

The café had a great product but no identity to set it apart from ten other cafés on the same street.

The solution

A warm identity inspired by heritage

We designed a logo, colors, type and packaging, plus a usage guide that keeps the brand consistent in every branch.
